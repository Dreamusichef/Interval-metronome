Result-Reveal Sandbox
Plays the real result screen with the exact game timings so you can plan sound effects. Rank animates in, then (after 1.0s) one trophy pops while the results blur. Tap / click / any key dismisses the trophy.
Press Play. Elapsed ms is shown for every phase — use it to time your SFX.

Animation reference (exact values from the game CSS/JS)

• Rank reveal — S / SS: rankBurst 0.75s ease cubic-bezier(.2,1.5,.4,1), after a 0.25s delay.
  A and below: rankPop 0.5s, same 0.25s delay.
• Gap before the trophy appears: 1000ms after the results show (JS timer).
• Results blur-back: filter 0.25s as the trophy starts.
• Trophy card in: tpPop 0.5s cubic-bezier(.2,1.5,.4,1) (3D pop).
• Trophy card out (on dismiss): tpOut 0.18s, then the next trophy or back to results.

SFX hook names already wired in the game: rank / rankS / rankSS (rank reveal) and trophyPop (each trophy).
